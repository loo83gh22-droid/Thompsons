# Photo Inbox

Drop any photos here and Claude will:
1. Rename them with a descriptive, SEO-friendly name
2. Sort them into the right category folder (family/, product/, founder/, lifestyle/)
3. Use them in future social media posts

## Categories
- **family/** - Real family moments (kids, holidays, cooking, trips, milestones)
- **product/** - App screenshots, feature highlights, UI cards
- **founder/** - Rob headshots, behind-the-scenes building the app
- **lifestyle/** - Generic warm/lifestyle imagery

## Naming Convention
`{description}-{number}.jpeg`
Examples: `fishing-dad-son-boat-1.jpeg`, `app-journal-entry-card-1.png`, `rob-at-desk-1.jpeg`

## How It Works
Once pushed to main, photos are publicly accessible at:
`https://familynest.io/marketing/social/{category}/{filename}`

Claude's social media tools use these URLs to publish to Facebook and Instagram.
