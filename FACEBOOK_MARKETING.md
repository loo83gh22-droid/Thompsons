### Post 6 — Rick Voicemail
**Status:** LIVE
**Theme:** The last voicemail you never deleted

---

You know that one voicemail you never deleted?

Mine is from my step dad, Rick. It ends the same way every message did. "Love you. Bye for now."

Bye for now. Not goodbye. He always said it like he'd be calling again soon.

He passed a few years ago. I still have the voicemail. But it lives in my phone, one software update away from disappearing.

That's part of why I built Family Nest. A place where that voicemail, that voice, that phrase he always used, doesn't have to live on borrowed time.

Does someone in your life have a phrase, a habit, a song, or just a way about them that's completely theirs?

Drop it in the comments. I'd love to hear it.

I have a billion photos on my phone.

Ok, not a billion. But it feels that way when you're scrolling back through years looking for that one picture.

I did find it. But it was just a picture. No story tied to it from when the memory happened.

Who was there? What were we laughing about? What did the day feel like?

That stuff gets a little fuzzy over time. The photo is still there but the story it carries is starting to fade.

Family Nest lets you add a voice note right to a photo, right when the memory is fresh. So the picture and the story stay together.

Your future self will thank you.

### Post 11 — Founder Story (March 18)
**Status:** Copy ready
**Theme:** Why Rob built Family Nest, why now
**Image:** Optional — photo of Rob or family

---

A few years ago I was sitting with my dad going through old photos on his phone.

He had thousands of them. No names, no context, no stories attached to any of them.

He pointed at one and said something like "that's from when your uncle came to visit." I asked which year. He wasn't sure.

I kept thinking about that after he passed. Not just the photos but everything around them. The stories. The people who knew what the photos meant.

There was nowhere to put all of that. Not really. Facebook is too public and too noisy. Google Photos keeps the pictures but loses the people. iCloud doesn't know who your grandmother is.

So I built something just for families. Private. Simple. A place for the things that actually matter.

If that sounds like something your family needs, it's at familynest.io.
