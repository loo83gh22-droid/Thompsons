## Voice and Copy Rules

- **Founder voice** — warm, personal, real. Not a brand. A person.
- **No em dashes** (the Facebook composer triggers a "Leave Page?" dialog with special characters)
- No corporate speak, no urgency tactics, no hard sell
- Conversational and open-ended — invite comments, not clicks
- CTAs are soft and warm, never pushy
- Typos in the composer are fine to fix before posting — always finalize copy in this doc first, then paste into Facebook

---

## Tech Notes for Facebook Posting

- **Workflow:** Meta Business Suite → Content Library → + Create → Post
- **Scheduling:** Set date/time → Schedule. To publish immediately: find the post → ··· menu → Reschedule → Publish now
- **"Leave Page?" dialog bug:** Typing em dashes or certain special characters in the composer triggers a navigation warning. Use "Keep editing" to dismiss. Avoid special characters entirely.
- **Timezone note:** Facebook scheduling may default to a different timezone than expected. Always double-check the scheduled time shown after saving.
- **Best posting times (North America):** 6:00 PM or 8:00 AM
- **Frequency:** 2 to 3 posts per week max at this stage
